# Contributing to Nums AI projects

<!-- SPDX-License-Identifier: Apache-2.0 -->

We welcome code and documentation contributions to Nums AI projects that adopt
this guide. Use the project's development documentation and CI for setup and
validation commands.

## Getting started

Search existing issues and pull requests. Small, focused changes can be submitted
directly. Discuss substantial features, breaking API changes, major refactors,
significant dependencies, or license changes in an issue before starting.
Keep each pull request focused and explain what changes and why.

## Preparing a pull request

- Add relevant tests for changed behavior and update affected documentation.
- Run relevant project checks. Describe the results and any checks you could not
  run.
- Provide a reproducible comparison for performance or accuracy claims.
- Explain new dependencies and identify the sources and licenses of third-party
  material.

Keep secrets, customer data, confidential records, caches, and large artifacts out
of pull requests. Small test fixtures are welcome when you have permission to
share them.

Before including non-public Nums AI material in a public issue, pull request, or
release, obtain explicit authorization from Nums AI. Discussion with maintainers
alone is not that authorization.

Review and understand your submission, including AI-assisted work. Do not send
confidential inputs to a service without authorization.

## Rights and review

By submitting an original contribution for inclusion in a project, you agree to
license it under the terms applicable to that material in the project, unless a
separate written contribution agreement applies. Under those terms, Nums AI may
use, modify, and distribute your contribution, including in commercial products
and services where permitted by the applicable license. Uses permitted by that
license do not require separate permission for each use.

You confirm that you have the rights and permissions needed to license your
contribution. Identify third-party material and preserve its licenses, notices,
and attribution. This policy does not transfer copyright ownership.

Code and documentation may have different license terms. Projects must identify
the applicable terms in their licensing or contribution documentation. Confirm
with maintainers before submission if those terms are unclear. Any additional
project requirements belong in that project's contribution guide.

Maintainers decide whether to accept a change based on its scope, correctness,
compatibility, validation, project goals, and maintenance capacity. Please respond
to review feedback. Submitting or discussing a contribution does not by itself
guarantee acceptance, a merge or release date, or ongoing support. Separately
stated support commitments remain in effect.

## Questions and reports

Use Issues for bugs and feature requests. Ask usage questions in Discussions when
enabled, or in Issues otherwise. Report vulnerabilities privately through
[Security](SECURITY.md), and conduct concerns through the
[Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
