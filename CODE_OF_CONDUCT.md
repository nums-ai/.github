# Nums AI Code of Conduct

<!-- SPDX-License-Identifier: CC-BY-SA-4.0 -->

## Scope

This Code applies to Nums AI open-source project communities that adopt it, including their repositories, communication channels, and events. It also applies when someone officially represents one of those communities, such as through an official project email address, social account, or appointed event role.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make our community welcoming, safe, and equitable for all.

We respect each person's dignity, rights, and contributions, regardless of race, ethnicity, caste, color, age, physical characteristics, neurodiversity, disability, sex or gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, language, philosophy or religion, national or social origin, socioeconomic position, education, or other status. We welcome good-faith participation and treat people respectfully and without discrimination.

## Encouraged behavior

Consider how culture, background, and language can affect how others understand your words and actions. Community members should:

- Respect the community's purpose, activities, and ways of gathering.
- Engage kindly and honestly, and respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Take responsibility for their actions and contributions.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Repair harm and support the community's well-being.

## Restricted behavior

Engaging in, threatening, or promoting any of the following violates this Code:

- **Harassment:** Violating expressed personal boundaries or continuing unwanted personal attention after a clear request to stop.
- **Personal attacks:** Insulting, demeaning, or disparaging a community member or group.
- **Stereotyping or discrimination:** Making derogatory generalizations about people based on their identity or background, or treating them unfairly on that basis.
- **Sexualization:** Behavior that is inappropriately intimate for the community's context or purpose.
- **Violating confidentiality:** Disclosing someone's private or sensitive information without authorization, or using personal information to harass, threaten, or endanger them. This rule does not prevent sharing relevant evidence privately with conduct maintainers to report or handle a concern in good faith.
- **Endangerment:** Causing, encouraging, or threatening violence or other harm toward a person or group.
- **Impersonation:** Posing as another person, including to evade enforcement.
- **Spam:** Repeated or disruptive unsolicited promotional content.
- Other conduct that causes or credibly threatens harm to participants or their ability to participate safely.

Good-faith disagreement or criticism of technical decisions, project governance, or Nums AI is not, by itself, a violation. The same behavior standards apply to how it is expressed.

## Reporting and handling

Project maintainers who handle conduct reports are called **conduct maintainers**. Report concerns privately to [trust@nums.world](mailto:trust@nums.world). Access to the mailbox should be limited to people handling reports. Keep reports and sensitive incident details out of public Issues, pull requests, and Discussions. Not every conflict is a Code violation, but concerns can still be raised privately.

Conduct maintainers should avoid conflicts of interest and, where practicable, ask a maintainer not involved in the incident to handle it. Reports may be seen by anyone with access to the mailbox. If your concern involves one of those people, request a private conversation before sharing sensitive incident details.

Conduct maintainers review reports fairly using relevant evidence and context. They handle reports discreetly and explain decisions where appropriate, taking safety and privacy into account.

A person subject to an enforcement decision may request reconsideration by email to [trust@nums.world](mailto:trust@nums.world), based on significant new information, a material factual or procedural error, or a disproportionate application of this Code. Available conduct maintainers assess the request using relevant evidence and seek input from a maintainer not involved in the incident or original decision where practicable. Reconsideration is an internal maintainer process; a separate independent reviewer is not guaranteed. Restrictions remain in effect during reconsideration.

## Enforcement

If an investigation establishes a violation, conduct maintainers consider its impact on the people involved and the community. This ladder guides their response; serious violations may justify skipping steps. Conduct maintainers exercise judgment based on the available evidence, the severity and impact of the conduct, and relevant context. Enforcement should be proportionate and consistent across similar cases.

1. **Warning:** A private written warning explaining the conduct and expected changes.
2. **Temporary activity limits:** Time-limited restrictions on particular channels or interactions for recurrence after a warning or a more serious first violation.
3. **Temporary suspension:** Suspension for repeated violations despite warnings or a single serious violation, with stated duration and conditions for return.
4. **Permanent ban:** Removal from all project community spaces, tools, and channels for repeated violations unresolved by earlier steps, or a violation that makes safe continued participation impossible. Bans should be rare and supported by strong reasons. A permanent ban has no routine path to reinstatement, but remains subject to the reconsideration process above.
